How This Calculator Works

Formula Breakdown
Step 1: Calculate Silver Needed
Pure Silver (grams) = (Target Concentration in g/L × Final Volume in mL) ÷ 1000

Example: For 500 mL at 150 g/L → (150 × 500) ÷ 1000 = 75 grams pure silver

Actual Material (grams) = Pure Silver ÷ (Purity % ÷ 100)

Example: For 92.5% purity → 75 ÷ 0.925 = 81.08 grams of material
Convert to troy oz: 81.08 g ÷ 31.1035 = 2.606 troy oz
Step 2: Calculate Nitric Acid Needed
Nitric for Silver = Pure Silver (g) × 1.17 × (70 ÷ Your Acid %)
Nitric for Base Metals = Impurities (g) × 4.15 × (70 ÷ Your Acid %)
Total Nitric Acid = Nitric for Silver + Nitric for Base Metals

The baseline is 1.17 mL of 70% nitric per gram of pure silver and 4.15 mL per gram of copper/base metals. This ensures complete dissolution with no free nitric remaining (saturated solution).

If using a different concentration, the amount is adjusted:
• 70% nitric: multiply by 1.0 (baseline)
• 50% nitric: multiply by 1.4 (need more volume)
• 90% nitric: multiply by 0.78 (need less volume)

Example: 75g pure silver + 6.08g impurities (81.08g at 92.5% purity) with 70% nitric:
• Silver: 75 × 1.17 × 1.0 = 88 mL
• Base metals: 6.08 × 4.15 × 1.0 = 25 mL
• Total: 113 mL nitric acid
Step 3: Calculate Distilled Water for Dissolution
Distilled Water for Dissolution = Nitric Acid (mL) × 1.3889

The ratio is approximately 45mL acid : 62mL water (1:1.39). This extra water accounts for evaporation during heating and provides better reaction conditions.

Example: 88 mL nitric × 1.3889 = 122 mL Distilled Water

Saturated Solution Volume = Nitric + Distilled Water = 88 + 122 = 210 mL
Step 4: Calculate Additional Distilled Water for Final Volume
Additional Distilled Water = Final Volume - Saturated Solution Volume

After dissolving the silver, dilute the saturated solution to your target final volume.

Example: 500 mL - 210 mL = 290 mL additional distilled water

Total Distilled Water Used = 122 + 290 = 412 mL
Key Constants Used:
• 1 troy oz = 31.1035 grams
• 1.17 mL of 70% HNO₃ per gram pure Ag (saturated solution)
• 4.15 mL of 70% HNO₃ per gram Cu/base metals
• 1:1.3889 ratio of acid to distilled water for dissolution (45:62)
• Concentration adjustment factor = 70 ÷ (your acid %)
• Purity adjustment = Pure Silver ÷ (Purity % ÷ 100)
Why These Ratios Matter:
The 1.17 mL per gram ratio creates a saturated solution with no free nitric acid. This is critical because free nitric will dissolve your silver anode during electrolysis instead of allowing proper plating. The electrolyte must be "pregnant" (fully saturated) with silver to function correctly.
Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Dissolve Silver
Add the nitric acid and distilled water shown above to your silver (ratio ~45:62 acid to water). Heat on a hot plate until reaction completes. The extra water accounts for evaporation during heating. If undissolved silver remains, add more acid in small increments. Once all silver is dissolved, add a small amount more silver to ensure all free nitric is consumed.
Step 2: Dilute to Final Volume
After dissolving the silver, add the additional distilled water shown to reach your target final volume. Mix thoroughly and verify the solution is fully saturated before use in your electrolytic cell.
